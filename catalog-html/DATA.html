Data Science
  

  • DATA 2402 - Programming for Data Scientists


    Credit(s): 3
    Lecture Hour(s): 3
    Lecture Hours Schedule Type: Lecture
    Tutorial Hour(s): 2

    This course reinforces the programming knowledge and introduces basic data structures and algorithms useful for data analysis. Students also learn important programming tools and frameworks for data analysis.

    Prerequisite(s): MATH 1203  and COMP 1701 , each with a minimum grade of C-.
    General Science Option
  

  • DATA 2721 - Data Science I: Introduction to Databases


    Credit(s): 3
    Lecture Hour(s): 3
    Lecture Hours Schedule Type: Lecture
    Tutorial Hour(s): 1

    This course emphasizes concepts necessary to design and implement databases using a variety of data models including relational, and non-relational models. Practical application with a number of Database Management Systems is included with an emphasis on data processing in a data science environment.

    Prerequisite(s): A grade of C- or higher in either COMP 1701  or COMP 2001 .
    Note: Credit will be allowed for only one of COMP 2521  and DATA 2721.

    General Science Option
  

  • DATA 3453 - Data Visualization


    Credit(s): 3
    Lecture Hour(s): 3
    Tutorial Hour(s): 1

    This course introduces principles of visual design, perceptual psychology, and cognitive science to guide students in creating clear and effective visualizations of complex data. Students will learn charting and data representation techniques using a variety of software tools. The goal is to equip students with the skills to produce sophisticated visualizations that clearly communicate insights in data.

    Prerequisite(s): DATA 2402  with minimum grade of C-.
  

  • DATA 3463 - Foundations of Data Acquisition


    Credit(s): 3
    Lecture Hour(s): 3
    Tutorial Hour(s): 1

    This course introduces techniques for obtaining data from databases, documents, the web, and other sources. Further topics include concepts of data quality and how to address quality issues. Data science process models, the critical role of data acquisition and labeling within the larger business process, and ethical considerations in collecting data are all emphasized.

    Prerequisite(s): DATA 2721  and DATA 2402  each with a minimum grade of C-.
  

  • DATA 3464 - Foundations of Data Processing


    Credit(s): 3
    Lecture Hour(s): 3
    Tutorial Hour(s): 1

    This course introduces techniques for ethically and responsibly wrangling and manipulating datasets to make them appropriate for addressing the question at hand. Topics may include cleaning and transforming data, integrity and quality measures, common file formats, feature selection and engineering, and generating features from unstructured sources such as text and images.

    Prerequisite(s): DATA 2402  and MATH 2444 , each with a minimum grade of C-.
    Recommended Preparation: DATA 3463  
  

  • DATA 3491 - Work Integrated Learning in Data Science


    Credit(s): 3
    Lecture Hour(s): .75
    Other Hour(s): 6
    Other Hours Schedule Type: Internship

    Students will apply their data science skills in a real-world work environment with a partner organization. They will tackle a data-related problem identified by the organization and develop a deliverable that aligns with the organization’s needs. Projects may involve tasks such as data collection, analysis, modeling, or interpretation, depending on the student’s background and the placement’s focus.

    Prerequisite(s): Completion of COOP 0001  and DATA 3463  with a minimum grade of C-.
    Note: The course will run three hours of lecture for first two weeks, with the rest of the semester being internship hours. Students will complete 60-80 Internship hours over the course of the semester. Students will have a lecture sesssion scheduled at the end of the semester for their final showcase.
  

  • DATA 4465 - Machine Learning


    Credit(s): 3
    Lecture Hour(s): 3
    Lecture Hours Schedule Type: Lecture
    Tutorial Hour(s): 1

    This course will teach how to implement and apply machine learning algorithms. The emphasis will be on practical skills, and the course will focus on giving students skills to make these algorithms work. Students will learn about commonly used learning techniques including supervised and unsupervised learning techniques. Performance evaluation and model selection techniques will be discussed in this course. Students will gain hands-on experience through course assignments and projects.

    Prerequisite(s): MATH 2303 , MATH 3552 , DATA 3453  and DATA 3463 , each with a minimum grade of C-.
  

  • DATA 4734 - Network Science: Techniques and Applications


    Credit(s): 3
    Lecture Hour(s): 3
    Tutorial Hour(s): 1

    This course offers an introduction to Network Science, emphasizing the theoretical foundations and practical methodologies for modeling and analyzing complex systems. Key topics include network metrics, algorithms for topological evolution, computational techniques for structural analysis, simulation of dynamics in networks, and machine learning-driven applications for robustness assessment and graph embedding.

    Prerequisite(s): MATH 2234 ; and one of MATH 1271  or MATH 2071 , and one of COMP 2631  or DATA 2402 , all with a minimum grade of C-.
  

  • DATA 5496 - Data Science Capstone


    Credit(s): 3
    This is a mentored project-based course on data and questions from a partner within or outside the university. Students may choose projects related to the concentration that they have chosen and work with a mentor with expertise in that domain. With consultation with their mentors, students will formulate questions and design and execute a suitable solution. Students will produce a project report, presentation, and possibly other products, such as a computer application to demonstrate the solution.

    Prerequisite(s): DATA 3453  and DATA 3464  both with a minimum grade of C- and department approval.
